Lanterna is a daily puzzle game. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does with your data.

No account, no tracking

Lanterna has no sign-up, no username, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not ask for your location, contacts, photos or microphone.

What stays on your device

Your puzzle results, settings and chosen home port are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, this progress is also stored in your private iCloud database so it follows you across your devices. We cannot read it: it belongs to your iCloud account.

What is sent to the shared world counters

When you finish the daily puzzle, the app adds your lanterns (1 to 4) to anonymous counters stored in a public CloudKit database: a world total, and per-port counters for the day and the week (port identifier, number of lanterns, number of players). These counters contain no name, no account identifier and no device identifier. Your home port is picked from a closed list of about 400 ports; it is not your location.

Sharing

When you share a result, the app creates an image or a text containing the puzzle number, your move count, your streak and a grid showing the shape of the route. Nothing personal is included. Your port is named only if you turn on that option in Settings. You decide whether and where to share.

Notifications

An optional reminder at 9 pm is a local notification scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment information.

Children

Lanterna contains no chat, no free text from players and no user-generated content.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. You can remove the iCloud copy in Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage. The anonymous counters cannot be traced back to you.
