Common questions

  • My lanterns do not reach my port. You need an internet connection and to be signed in to iCloud. Pending results are sent automatically later.
  • The daily puzzle changed. A new puzzle starts every day at midnight UTC, the same for everyone.
  • Restore a purchase. Open Journal > Shop > Restore.
  • Change my port. Settings > Home port > Change. Future lanterns go to the new port.
